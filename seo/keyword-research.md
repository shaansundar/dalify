## 7. Technical SEO Notes

- **Canonical tags**: Ensure `/collections/organic-dal` is canonical over any filtered URLs like `/collections/organic-pulses?type=dal`
- **Hreflang**: Not needed initially (India-only store)
- **Image ALT text pattern**: `organic [product name] [quantity] - Dalify`
- **Schema markup**: Implemented in `src/lib/seo/structured-data.ts` — `Product`, `CollectionPage`, `WebSite`, `Organization`, `FAQPage`, `AboutPage`, `ContactPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `ItemList` schemas are live. Verify via Google Rich Results Test after launch.
- **Page speed**: Target LCP < 2.5s on mobile — use Next.js `<Image>` with WebP, leverage Shopify CDN for product images.
- **Sitemap**: Auto-generated by `src/app/sitemap.ts` at `dalify.in/sitemap.xml` — submit to GSC on launch day.
- **Robots.txt**: `src/app/robots.ts` blocks `/api/`, `/admin`, `/cart`, `/checkout`, `/account`, `/search`, `/_next/`.
- **Google Search Console**: Verify property on launch day, submit sitemap, monitor Index Coverage
