## Ordering & Products

### What products does Dalify sell?

Dalify sells certified organic spices, pulses, grains, and instant mixes for the Indian kitchen. Our range covers everyday staples like turmeric, cumin, and moong dal, as well as regional specialities and convenient instant mixes for idli, dosa, upma, and more. Browse our full catalogue on the [Shop page](/collections/all).

### Are your products really organic?

Yes. All Dalify products are sourced from farms certified under India's National Programme for Organic Production (NPOP) or equivalent FSSAI-recognised certification bodies. We conduct annual audits and maintain traceability from farm to pack. Certifications are available on request — just [contact us](/pages/contact).

### Do you add any preservatives, colours, or flavour enhancers?

Never. Our products contain only what nature put in them. No preservatives, no artificial colours, no anti-caking agents, no flavour enhancers. The ingredient list on every Dalify product is exactly one ingredient long.

### What size packs are available?

Most products are available in 250g, 500g, and 1kg options. A few seasonal or specialty items come in 100g trial packs. Pack sizes are clearly listed on each product page.

### Do you offer wholesale or bulk orders?

We currently focus on direct-to-consumer orders. If you're interested in bulk quantities for a restaurant, cloud kitchen, or retail arrangement, please [write to us](mailto:hello@dalify.in) and we'll work something out.

## Shipping & Delivery

### Where do you deliver?

We deliver across India — all states and union territories covered through our logistics partner Shiprocket. We currently do not ship internationally, but this is on our roadmap.

### How long does delivery take?

- **Metro cities** (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 2–3 business days
- **Tier-2 cities**: 3–5 business days
- **Remote or rural areas**: 5–7 business days

These are estimates. Actual delivery may vary based on location and seasonal demand.

### How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link via email and SMS. You can also log into your Dalify account and view order status under **My Orders**. For any tracking issues, email us at [support@dalify.in](mailto:support@dalify.in).

### What is the shipping charge?

Shipping is **free on orders above ₹499**. For orders below ₹499, a flat shipping fee of ₹49 applies.

### Do you offer express or same-day delivery?

Currently we offer standard delivery only. Express delivery options are being explored for select cities — subscribe to our newsletter to be notified when it's available.

### What if I'm not available to receive my order?

Our courier will attempt delivery twice. If both attempts fail, the package is returned to our facility. We'll contact you to arrange a re-delivery (re-delivery fee may apply) or a refund.

## Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:
- **UPI** (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, all UPI apps)
- **Credit & Debit Cards** (Visa, Mastercard, Rupay, Amex)
- **Net Banking** (all major Indian banks)

### Is my payment information secure?

Yes. All payments are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway. We do not store any card or UPI information on our servers.

### Is Cash on Delivery available everywhere?

COD is available in most serviceable pin codes for orders up to ₹2,000. If COD is not available for your pin code, it will not appear as a payment option at checkout.

### I was charged but my order wasn't placed. What do I do?

If your payment was debited but you didn't receive an order confirmation email, the amount will typically be auto-reversed to your account within 5–7 business days. If it isn't, email us at [support@dalify.in](mailto:support@dalify.in) with your payment reference number and we'll resolve it promptly.

### What is your return policy?

We accept returns within **7 days of delivery** for:

Opened products can be returned if you believe there is a quality issue — we take every such report seriously.

### How do I initiate a return?

Email [support@dalify.in](mailto:support@dalify.in) with:
1. Your order number
2. A brief description of the issue

Refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** after we receive and inspect the returned item. The amount is refunded to your original payment method. UPI and card refunds may take an additional 2–3 days to reflect depending on your bank.

### What if my order arrived damaged?

Please email us at [support@dalify.in](mailto:support@dalify.in) with your order number and a photo of the damaged package within 48 hours of delivery. We'll send a replacement or issue a full refund — your choice.

## Product Quality & Certifications

### How do I know your products are genuinely organic?

We work exclusively with NPOP-certified farms and FSSAI-licensed processing facilities. Our packaging includes batch codes linked to farm-level records. If you ever want to verify the certification for a specific product, email us with the batch code and we'll provide documentation.

### What is NPOP?

NPOP stands for National Programme for Organic Production — India's official organic certification system administered by APEDA under the Ministry of Commerce. It is equivalent to EU and USDA organic standards.

### Do your products contain allergens?

Our facility handles various grains, pulses, and tree nuts. While we maintain strict hygiene protocols, cross-contamination cannot be completely ruled out. Each product page lists applicable allergen advisories. If you have a severe allergy, please [contact us](/pages/contact) before ordering.

### How should I store your products?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. For opened packets, transfer to an airtight container. Most spices retain optimal flavour for 12–18 months; pulses and grains for 12–24 months. Best before dates are printed on all packs.
