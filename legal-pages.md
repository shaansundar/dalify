# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

Dalify Foods Pvt. Ltd. ("Dalify", "we", "us", or "our") operates the website dalify.in and related mobile applications. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

This policy is compliant with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and reflects best practices aligned with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Information We Collect

**Information you provide directly:**
- Name, email address, phone number
- Delivery address and billing address
- Payment details (processed securely via Razorpay; we do not store card numbers)
- Account credentials (if you create an account)
- Communications you send us (emails, WhatsApp messages, contact form submissions)

**Information collected automatically:**
- Device and browser type, operating system
- IP address and approximate geographic location
- Pages visited, time spent, referring URLs
- Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 6)

**Information from third parties:**
- Payment processors (transaction confirmation, not card details)
- Logistics partners (delivery status and address verification)
- Social media platforms (if you connect your account or interact with our social pages)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

- Process and fulfil your orders
- Send order confirmation, shipping, and delivery notifications
- Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
- Process returns, refunds, and warranty claims
- Improve our website, products, and services
- Send marketing communications (with your consent, and you may opt out at any time)
- Detect and prevent fraud or misuse
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the EU/EEA, we process personal data on the following legal bases:
- **Contract performance**: to process orders and deliver products
- **Legitimate interests**: to improve our services and prevent fraud
- **Consent**: for marketing communications
- **Legal obligation**: to comply with applicable laws

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share information only with:

- **Logistics partners** (e.g., Shiprocket, courier companies): to deliver your orders
- **Payment processors** (Razorpay): to process payments securely
- **Email service providers** (e.g., Klaviyo): to send transactional and marketing emails
- **Analytics providers** (e.g., Google Analytics): to understand website usage (anonymised data)
- **Legal authorities**: when required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights

All third-party service providers are bound by data processing agreements and are required to maintain the security of your information.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations (typically 7 years for financial records under Indian law), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us at privacy@dalify.in. Certain data may be retained for legal compliance even after deletion.

## 6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for:
- **Essential cookies**: required for the website to function (cart, login)
- **Analytics cookies**: to understand how visitors use our site
- **Marketing cookies**: to serve relevant advertising (with consent)

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie consent tool. Disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Delete** your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restrict** processing of your data
- **Data portability** (receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format)
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** for marketing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@dalify.in. We will respond within 30 days.

Indian residents may additionally contact the Adjudicating Officer under the IT Act, 2000.

## 8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmissions
- Encrypted storage of sensitive data
- Access controls limiting employee access to personal data
- Regular security audits

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

# Refund and Return Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

We want you to be completely satisfied with every Dalify purchase. This policy explains when and how you can return products and receive a refund.

## Eligibility for Returns

You may return a product within **7 days of the delivery date** if:

1. The product arrived damaged or broken
2. The product is defective (spoiled, contaminated, discoloured, off-odour)
3. You received the wrong product
4. The product significantly differs from its description

We also accept returns on **opened products** if you have a genuine quality concern — just contact us and describe the issue.

**Not eligible for returns:**
- Change of mind after opening
- Products returned after 7 days without prior communication
- Products damaged due to improper storage after delivery

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Email **support@dalify.in** with your order number and the issue
2. Attach photos of the product (required for damaged/defective claims)
3. Our team will respond within **24 hours**
4. If approved, we'll arrange a free pickup from your delivery address
5. Refund is processed once the product is received and inspected

## Refund Timeline

| Refund Method | Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Original payment method (card/UPI) | 5–7 business days after return receipt |
| Cash on Delivery orders | Bank transfer within 7–10 business days |
| Dalify store credit | Instant upon return approval |

Refunds may take an additional 2–3 days to reflect in your account depending on your bank or payment provider.

## Replacements

If you prefer a replacement over a refund, we'll ship the replacement product once we've arranged pickup of the original. Replacements are dispatched within 2 business days of return approval.

## Partial Refunds

If only part of your order is eligible for a refund, we'll refund only the affected items. Shipping charges are refunded only if the entire order is returned due to our error.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned:
- Products that have been fully consumed
- Gift cards and promotional credits
- Products explicitly marked "Final Sale"

For return-related queries: support@dalify.in or WhatsApp +91-XXXXXXXXXX

# Shipping Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

## Order Processing

Orders are processed Monday to Saturday. Orders placed before **2:00 PM IST** on a business day are typically dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM or on Sundays and public holidays are dispatched the next business day.

You will receive a dispatch confirmation email with tracking details once your order ships.

## Delivery Timelines

| Location | Estimated Delivery |
|---|---|
| Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata) | 2–3 business days |
| Tier-2 cities | 3–5 business days |
| Tier-3 cities and towns | 4–6 business days |
| Remote and rural areas | 5–7 business days |

These are estimates. Actual delivery may vary due to weather, carrier delays, or other circumstances beyond our control.

## Shipping Charges

- **Free shipping** on all orders above **₹499**
- **₹49 flat shipping fee** on orders below ₹499

Shipping fees (if any) are displayed at checkout before payment.

## Tracking Your Order

Track your order via:
- The tracking link in your dispatch email
- The **My Orders** section of your Dalify account
- Directly on the carrier website using the AWB/tracking number provided

## Delivery Attempts

Our courier will make up to 2 delivery attempts. If both fail:
- The package is returned to our facility
- We will contact you to reschedule delivery (₹49 re-delivery fee) or issue a refund (excluding original shipping fee)

Ensure your delivery address and phone number are accurate at the time of ordering.

## Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD is available for orders up to **₹2,000** in eligible pin codes. COD availability is shown at checkout. We are not responsible for failed COD delivery attempts due to customer unavailability.

## Damaged Shipments

If your order arrives visibly damaged, please:
1. Photograph the package before opening
2. Open in front of the delivery executive if possible
3. Contact us at support@dalify.in within **48 hours of delivery**

We will arrange a replacement or refund promptly.

## Shipping Restrictions

We currently ship to all serviceable pin codes within India. We do not ship internationally at this time. Certain remote areas or restricted zones may not be serviceable — you will be notified at checkout if your pin code is not currently serviceable.

## Contact

For shipping-related queries: support@dalify.in
